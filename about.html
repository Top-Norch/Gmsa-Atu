About GIMSA-ATU

The Ghana Muslim Students' Association at ATU is a community for Muslim students committed to promoting Islamic conciousness,brotherhood,personal development,academic excellence among Muslim students on campus, and support one another through university life.

Faith, knowledge, community

A place to grow together

GIMSA-ATU brings students together around shared Islamic values, learning, fellowship, and service on campus.

01 / PURPOSE

Our Purpose

To unite and support Muslim students at ATU while promoting Islamic values,education,leadership,brotherhood and service.

02 / VISION

Our Vision

To build a united,knowledgeable,responsible and service-oriented Muslim student community at Accra Technical University.

What guides us

Our Core Values

01

Faith

Keep Islamic principles at the heart of how we learn, grow, and care for one another.

02

Knowledge

Value the pursuit of sound Islamic understanding and academic excellence.

03

Brotherhood & Sisterhood

Build a welcoming community grounded in respect, belonging, and mutual support.

04

Service

Use our time and abilities to contribute positively to ATU and the wider community.

05

Leadership

Develop and nurture effective leaders who embody Islamic values and contribute meaningfully to the university and society.

06

Excellence

Strive for excellence in all our endeavors, reflecting the highest standards of Islamic conduct and academic achievement.
